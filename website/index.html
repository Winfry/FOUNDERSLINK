How it works

One place for the whole deal. Whoever you are.

Founders raise money. Investors find businesses that fit what they fund. Experts help both sides get it done properly.

For founders

Find investors who fit your business.

For founders raising money in Kenya.

  1. Tell us about your businessIn your own words, in English or Kiswahili. We fill in the form for you to check.
  2. See who fits, and whyInvestors in three lists: Ready, After a fix and Not a fit, each with plain reasons.
  3. Get readyA short checklist shows what investors will ask for, and what to fix first.
  4. Connect and closeOnce you are verified, send a request. When you both agree, open a deal room.
Join as a founder
For investors

See only the businesses you fund.

For angels, funds, syndicates and accelerators.

  1. Say what you fundSectors, stages, counties and the size of cheque you write.
  2. Get verifiedA person at FoundersLink checks you and your organisation.
  3. Meet founders who fitEach one checked, with the reasons for the fit and how ready they are.
  4. Make an offerSend a request with the amount you have in mind. Talk once it is accepted, then open a deal room.
Join as an investor
For experts

Help founders when it matters.

For advocates, accountants, valuers, surveyors, mentors and more.

  1. Tell us your professionAnd the register you are on, such as LSK or ICPAK.
  2. We check you thereA person confirms you on that register before anyone sees your profile.
  3. Set your ratesA fixed price, per hour, or free. Founders see them before they ask.
  4. Get found at the right momentFounders reach you when a deal needs you, and pay you directly.
