Questions

Good questions. Plain answers.

For founders

No. FoundersLink is free for founders.

Yes. Straight after you join you see which investors fit and why: what they fund, their range and the reasons. Their names show once you are verified.

The investor fits your business, but asks for a paper you do not have yet, such as your CR12. Get it, tick it in Getting ready, and they move to Ready.

Yes. The app works in English and Kiswahili, and you can describe your business in your own words, Sheng included.

For investors

Angel investors, angel networks and syndicates, venture capital funds, impact funds, and accelerators that invest.

Before you can contact founders, a person at FoundersLink checks you, including your organisation and website. Founders' names are hidden from you until then.

Founders whose business fits what you told us you fund: your sectors, stages, counties and cheque size. Each comes with the reasons for the fit and how ready they are.

Send a request with a short note and the amount you have in mind. If the founder accepts, you can talk, and open a deal room with those terms.

For experts

No. Joining is free for experts.

Seventeen kinds, among them advocates, company secretaries, accountants, tax agents, valuers, land surveyors, architects, engineers, insurance brokers, mentors and business consultants. Legal, tax and audit work is offered only by those licensed to do it.

A person at FoundersLink checks you against an independent source before members see you: your professional register (for example LSK or ICPAK), the body that awarded your qualification, or your work history and references. Your profile says which, in words: Licensed, Certified or Experience checked.

You set your own rates: a fixed price, per hour, or free. Founders pay you directly. FoundersLink never holds the money.

Money and safety

Never. There is no wallet, balance or escrow. Money moves between you through your own bank. FoundersLink keeps a record of the deal.

FoundersLink never asks for or stores ID numbers or ID documents. Where identity is checked, a regulated identity provider does it and tells us only whether the check passed.

A message that asks for money carries a warning. Never send money outside a closed deal. You can report or block anyone at any time.

Nobody can honestly promise that. What we promise is that everyone you talk to has been checked by a person, and that you can report anyone.
