The founder's desk. Calm, dense, and serious.

04 — The system

Fewer colours. Lighter type. Real people.

The look relies on contrast, space and photography, not decoration. Gold stays as the one accent, used for a single detail per screen. Purple stays in the logo only.

What goes

  • 01The cartoon people and drawn scenes
  • 02The beadwork pattern and striped bar
  • 03Purple backgrounds and coloured cards
  • 04Heavy, bold headings (weight 800)
  • 05Icon-led tabs and buttons

What stays

  • 01Words on every tab and button
  • 02Plain sentences: why an investor fits, what to fix
  • 03Progress as a count ("4 of 7"), never a score
  • 04Honest labels: verified, AI, "FoundersLink never holds money"
  • 05Kiswahili, and a larger-text setting
