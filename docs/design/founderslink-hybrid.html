A new member joins. Then lands where everyone else does.

What someone sees the very first time they open FoundersLink. They give only the details that matter, and are then taken to the same Home an existing member has. 03 — Web app

The same app on a computer. Just wider.

On a computer, each member sees exactly what the phone shows: the purple band with the greeting, then the same cards in the same order and the same words. The phone's bottom tabs become a side menu in words, with a count beside each place, and the cards spread over two columns. No empty margins. Click Overview, Investors and Messages in the side menu to open their pages. Choose First time to see a new founder or investor join on a computer, from the handshake welcome to their Home.

04 — The hybrid

What came from where. And what was left behind.

The first design had the right heart: plain words, a guide, both roles, Kiswahili. The second had the right face: purple and gold, light type, pill buttons, real photos. This takes each one's strengths and drops what made them childish or cold.
