04 — The hybrid

What came from where. And what was left behind.

The first design had the right heart: plain words, a guide, both roles, Kiswahili. The second had the right face: purple and gold, light type, pill buttons, real photos. This takes each one's strengths and drops what made them childish or cold.

PartSourceHow it looks now
Voice and wordsFirst designGreetings by name, reasons in full sentences, "Your next step" with how long it takes. Words on every tab and button.
InteractionsFirst designFounder and investor views, Kiswahili, larger text, the guide, My investments, check-ins and chamas, all kept.
ColourSecond designDeep purple for the top bar and sidebar, FoundersLink purple for actions, warm paper behind, gold for one detail per screen.
TypeSecond designInter Tight: light for big headings and numbers, medium for names and buttons. No more heavy bold (800).
PeopleSecond designReal photos where we have them, clean initials in a lavender circle where we don't. No cartoon faces.
ProgressNewThe gold beads became a thin segmented bar, still read in words: "4 of 7".
The AI guideNewA gold circle marked "AI" instead of a drawn diamond, so it reads as a tool, not a person.
Left behindBothCartoon people and scenes, the beadwork pattern, coloured cards everywhere, black-and-white filters, and the dark theme that did not match the brand.
