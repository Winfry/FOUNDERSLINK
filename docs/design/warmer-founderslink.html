Fewer icons. More people, and plain words.

A proposal to make FoundersLink feel like a person helping you, not a control panel. It is built for founders, investors and experts of every age, including those who don't read app icons.

Click through the phone below. Every button works, and you can switch it to Kiswahili or make the text larger.

What changes

Five rules for a warmer app

Grown-ups shouldn't have to guess what a little picture means. These rules apply to every screen.

1

Words, not symbols

Every button and tab says what it does: "Messages", "Ask to connect", "Mark as done". There are no icon-only buttons anywhere.

2

Faces, not pictograms

Investors, experts and chama members show as people with faces and names. A person is easier to trust than a logo.

3

One next step

Home tells you the one thing to do now, how long it takes, and why. The full list is a tap away.

4

Talk like a person

Greetings by name, reasons in full sentences, and a guide you can just ask, in English or Kiswahili.

5

Big and easy to read

Larger text by default, a "Larger text" setting, and buttons at least a thumb tall, with strong contrast.

How I would build it

In this order, smallest risk first

Each step can ship on its own, and the backend doesn't change. This is all in the mobile app.

1

Words on every tab and button

Replace the icon tab bar and the bell and sparkle buttons with words. Rename tabs: Home, Investors, Get ready, Guide, Chamas.

2

A "Larger text" setting

Raise body text to 16–18, add Settings → Text size, and save it on the member like the language.

3

Faces for people

Ask for a profile photo at sign-up, with a friendly drawn face (matching skin tones) until one is added.

4

Home as "your next step"

One next step with time and reason, the bead progress, and investors as people.

5

The Guide as a tab

The Co-pilot moves into the tab bar, with ready-made questions to tap. Speaking a question comes later.

6

More screens in Kiswahili

Today only readiness items and the Co-pilot follow the language. Next: tabs, buttons and headings.

7

My investments, for investors

The list of closed deals, their goals and the track record can be built now, because the backend already has milestones and track records. Check-ins at 30, 90 and 180 days and founder updates need new backend work.
